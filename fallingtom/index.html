Falling Tom Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026

Falling Tom is an arcade game developed by Blaz Apps: you tilt your phone to steer Tom as he falls. The game is private by default — there is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. Your scores, achievements, and play history stay on your device.

1. Data Stored On Your Device

Falling Tom stores the following data locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Best distance, achievements, tutorial progress, and your selected skin.
  • A log of finished runs: distance, duration, in-game event counts (balloons, clouds, power-ups), the skin worn, a timestamp, and the run's random seed.

None of this identifies you, and the app never uploads it. It exists so your progress, achievements, and history screens work.

2. Android Auto Backup

The app's saved data is included in Android's automatic backup, so your progress survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. Backups are managed and encrypted by Android and stored with your Google account; you control them in your device's backup settings. Blaz Apps has no access to these backups.

3. Sensors and Haptics

The game reads the accelerometer in real time to steer Tom. Readings are used only for control, on your device, and are not recorded or transmitted. Vibration is used for haptic feedback on in-game events and involves no data.

4. Network Use

Once per launch, the app makes one small request to a Google server (www.google.com/generate_204) solely to read the response's standard Date header. This anchors run timestamps to reliable time even if the device clock is set wrong. The request carries no personal or gameplay data; as with any web request, the server sees routine connection details such as your IP address, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The game works fully offline — without a connection, the device clock is used instead.

5. Advertising

Falling Tom shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example, to revive a run) and occasional interstitial ads between games. There are no banner ads during play.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, and ad-interaction data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required by law (for example, in the EEA and UK), a consent prompt is shown before personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's privacy settings.

6. No Analytics or Tracking

Falling Tom contains no analytics SDKs and no crash-reporting services, and we do not sell your data. Apart from ad serving described above, no data is shared with third parties.

7. Permissions Summary

  • Internet — the once-per-launch time anchor and ad serving.
  • Vibration — haptic feedback.
  • Accelerometer — tilt steering (declared as required hardware).

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Backup copies are managed by Android Auto Backup and can be removed through your device's backup settings. Because Blaz Apps holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

9. Children's Privacy

Falling Tom is not directed to children under 13 or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the effective date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
